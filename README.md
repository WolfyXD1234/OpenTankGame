# OpenTankGame
A rouguelike tank auto-battler

z, space, or enter to select

stat shorthand

hp : hpc
hp max : hpm
damage : dmg
damage range : drg
prefered range : prg
ok range : org
fire prefernce : frc
hit rate : htr
armor : arm
travel : spd


combat

{your tank}             {enemy tank}
{your hp}                 {enemy hp}
{distance}

random tank chosen to go first
A tank will either chose to fire or move baed on range and fire prefrence
backspace or q to retreat from combat


repair

lets you see the stats of your tank as well as repairing it


editer

where you change the parts of the tank
backspace or q to trash selected part
